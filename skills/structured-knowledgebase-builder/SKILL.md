# Structured Knowledgebase Builder

Own the reader's actual experience: useful understanding, judgment, action or retrieval, grounded in sources. A responsible author/editor reads and uses the current deliverable, chooses what belongs, and integrates specialist advice. Reports and model passes cannot take that responsibility.

## Work at the requested endpoint

Reuse confirmed reader goals, product posture, sources and authorization. Diagnose or design without starting production state. A sample stays a sample; a local package does not authorize external publication. Do not ask again for an already settled direction.

For an explanatory product, write the consequential explanation in the existing manuscript or design, not only a statement of its intended thesis. Develop its reasons, reasonable alternatives and changing conditions enough to guide research and selection. Organize structure, order and emphasis around that work, then concentrate on expression and presentation. Research, actual passages and diagrams may revise the thinking throughout; these are connected activities rather than frozen stages or approval checkpoints. Keep valid work when revising a decision.

For a course, book or substantial restructuring, use the architect's [structural method](../knowledgebase-architect/references/structure-method.md). Develop the reader's progression before fixing chapter divisions; inspect the opening and consequential transitions in actual prose. Treat a failed entry point or cross-chapter argument at the product/part level instead of repeatedly expanding each chapter. A local prose improvement is evidence for that local change, not for the whole reading route.

Use the content builder's [shared editorial method](../knowledgebase-content-builder/references/editorial-method.md) for explanatory writing and review. Develop an important, defensible judgment through the actual article, choosing an explanation level that serves this reader. [Continuous comparisons](../knowledgebase-content-builder/references/editorial-examples.md) show why an accurate, complete procedure can still miss an article's purpose, and when operational details must remain.

Maintain current editorial intent, content relationships, accepted choices and unresolved questions in the existing authoring task/design document. Keep relevant original user questions and thought inputs traceable alongside the editor's interpretation, so a handoff does not silently narrow the assignment. Their claims may be revised through reasoning; they are not required conclusions. Keep source evidence at its owning location and preserve uncertainty and teaching assumptions. Ordinary work needs no separate model, learning, coverage, migration or self-review documents.

When feedback arrives, locate its cause in the question, structure, example, mechanism or expression before editing. Adding, deleting, merging, moving and replacing are all valid responses. Integrate advice rather than concatenating every suggestion. Material disagreements belong in the current editorial decision, not an endless loop seeking model consensus.

## Use only the relevant capability

- `knowledgebase-auditor`: source comprehension or an existing corpus problem; a full inventory is for an actual corpus audit/migration.
- `knowledgebase-architect`: connected questions, ownership and routes; adapt them when the actual content reveals a better structure.
- `knowledgebase-content-builder`: clear, substantive prose and examples, with the main author retaining continuity.
- `knowledgebase-learning-reviewer`: a current learning/progression/transfer question, not every article labeled for beginners.
- `knowledgebase-visual-explainer` and `knowledgebase-app-builder`: required explanatory assets and the requested presentation.
- `knowledgebase-qc-release`: current reading, functional and delivery checks at the requested scope.

For system explanations, the content builder's `references/novice-mental-model.md` helps diagnose causal jumps. For evidence-heavy material, use its `references/evidence-to-judgment.md`. These are editorial tools, not universal page templates.

## Execution and evidence

Use [scoped work](references/incremental-updates.md) for ordinary production and maintenance. `kb_update.py plan/check` schema 3 works with actual manuscript, existing intent/sources and one current editorial decision. It does not initialize or advance an eight-stage workflow. Completed independent editorial execution supplies its own decision; do not duplicate it in a page self-certificate or stage report.

Arrange [independent execution](references/independent-execution.md) when a current doubt, impact or explicit request warrants it. Beginner status and resolved historical feedback are not permanent triggers; an unresolved question about the whole approach remains current until relevant evidence addresses it. A reader can challenge value, premise, examples and progression as well as missing steps. The editor decides what to adopt using the current artifact. Further review is for a remaining question, not to force models to agree.

When the current doubt concerns overall arrangement, give the review the actual ordered `readingPath` plus the full passages needed to test it. Its overall structure finding remains separate from chapter findings within the same execution record. Select this scope deliberately; do not add an unrelated structural review to a spelling or isolated reference edit.

For core development or substantial rewriting where original intent matters, select relevant original excerpts through `intentSources` and explicit `readerTasks` in the same batch. Ask for the explanation, comparison or decision needed by this reader; do not copy the reader profile as a task or prescribe the desired verdict. Supply the complete prose unit needed to attempt it. The reader's actual work informs `intentReview` and the editor's decision. Keep author rationale and repair answers private. The program checks selection, delivery and evidence, not whether the selected work is a good test of the whole purpose.

Declared independent work must actually run. Relevant source/text/intent changes invalidate its evidence; missing or incomplete runs cannot be replaced by authored pass records. A consequential unresolved finding stops completion of its scope and dependent work. Unrelated work may continue. Program checks protect actual execution, version binding, arithmetic, local links, declared visual/interaction integrity and release hygiene; they do not certify useful writing or observed learning.

Existing staged projects remain readable under their old contract. Continue the [legacy staged workflow](references/workflow-protocol.md) only when it serves the assignment or is explicitly requested. A scoped change can coexist with a stale global verdict without claiming a global pass. Review-policy migration is explicit and never grants completion to old content.

Report the requested outcome, the actual editor judgment, checks performed and material limits. Preserve the current decision, relevant sources and unfinished work for continuation; do not make the reader product display internal controls.
