# Operations Mode

Start from recurring deliverables. Record trigger, owner, inputs, decisions, output, acceptance, handoff, and exceptions.

An executable SOP answers:

1. What outcome must be produced?
2. When is the procedure used or not used?
3. Which inputs, permissions, tools, and preconditions are required?
4. What are the steps and decisions?
5. What exactly is delivered and where?
6. How is quality checked?
7. Which exceptions and escalation paths exist?

Replace “根据实际情况判断” with observable signals, if/then rules, matrices, counterexamples, confidence, and escalation triggers.

Templates must be directly usable and include use condition, fields, definitions, a completed example, and validation. Metrics must define calculation, source, time window, exclusions, interpretation, and limitations. Label internal thresholds as defaults.
