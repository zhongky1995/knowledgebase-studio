# Knowledge Base QC Checklist

## Architecture And Allocation

- Mode and main path match actual reader jobs.
- Product posture, default action, first success, catalog visibility, exercise policy, progress semantics, pressure policy, and source presentation match the current contract.
- Important concepts, rules, metrics, templates, and cases have one canonical home.
- Migration actions are accounted for and public navigation uses new canonical paths.
- Internal planning and archives are not public.
- Important source claims, mechanisms, boundaries, conflicts, and unknowns exist in the internal understanding ledger.
- Every important knowledge unit traces to an understood source or explicitly labeled internal standard and has one canonical owner.
- Source-file count, learner-visible unit count, continuous-course count, references, labs, workbooks, and compatibility routes are reported separately.
- Every main-path learning unit appears exactly once in a progression and after its prerequisites.

## Usefulness And Readability

- Opening sections provide a useful answer, model, action, or output.
- Pages have one primary mental job.
- Meta narration, generic importance, repeated premise, case setup, bridges, and empty recaps are absent.
- Necessary mechanisms and boundaries remain after compression.
- Headings carry meaning; paragraphs avoid mixed reasoning walls.
- Formatting supports reasoning without text walls or bullet-list confetti.
- Terms are defined before use and prerequisites are linked rather than retaught.
- Essential source meanings survive compression; intentional changes and unresolved meanings are recorded.
- Customized openings do not conceal a shared legacy body, near-duplicate article, or forced common outline.
- Each page has a distinctive reader job and cannot be replaced by a neighboring page without losing value.

## Learning

- Reader-led products allow immediate reading without a task, artifact, curriculum decision, or completion commitment.
- Guided and practice-led products form an ability path with the outputs promised by the contract.
- High-leverage concepts have minimum sufficient mechanism and application depth.
- Key abilities have realistic practice and feedback.
- A representative reader can transfer the model to a new case.
- Required worked examples follow their declared pattern: judgment/revision examples expose attempts and reasoned changes; mechanism traces expose concrete participants, intermediate actions, outcome, boundary and changed condition. A template or topic list alone does not satisfy either.
- Every lesson has an explicit visual decision. Required visuals perform one declared learning job, correct one misconception, and preserve source mechanisms and boundaries.
- Static visuals are legible and embedded. Animated visuals show a meaningful state change, retain an accessible static fallback, and work with reduced motion.
- Advertised reading time is plausible for the prose, tables, decisions, and practice actually shown.

## Novice Experience

- In five seconds, a representative reader can state what this is, what to do next, and what commitment is expected.
- The default screen does not expose the full catalog, competing primary actions, control metadata, or progress machinery unless the contract requires them.
- Optional practice is visibly optional and cannot block reading, lookup, or navigation.
- Reader first screens contain useful content before duplicated summaries, taxonomy, or support UI.
- Psychological pressure is assessed explicitly; task language, deadlines, status labels, and empty progress states match the pressure policy.

## Operations

- Tasks identify trigger, owner, inputs, steps, decisions, outputs, quality, exceptions, and handoffs.
- Templates are usable and metrics are completely defined.
- A representative task can be completed without undocumented assumptions.

## Evidence

- Facts, inferences, hypotheses, and internal defaults are distinct.
- Material claims have current support.
- Cases are labeled real, anonymized, composite, or fictional.

## App And Release

- Links, routes, builds, search indexes, and generated content are current.
- The QC report records the current contract digest and deliverable fingerprint; newer inputs make the result stale.
- Desktop/mobile primary paths work without overflow or console blockers.
- Visible primary and direct route entries are actionable by pointer and keyboard and reach useful content within the approved click depth.
- The package has a clear entrypoint and excludes internal/control/archive/cache/secret material.
- Share form, audience scope, release root, external-publication authorization, and license policy match the contract.
- Public-ready packages are built in an isolated root; the full workspace is never the release artifact.
- Markdown tables are semantic tables, images load, relative article links resolve, and a label such as “全库搜索” really searches the full default scope.
- Declared visual assets, previews, posters, fallbacks, and alternative text survive packaging and work at desktop and mobile widths.
- Desktop and mobile checks run against the packaged result, not only the source app.
- Learner-facing output contains no internal paths, source ledgers, migration metadata, test reports, or maintenance-only fields.
- A public package has a user-approved license policy; QC never invents CC, MIT, or another license.
- Local release readiness is recorded separately from repository creation, upload, hosting, sending, and visibility changes.
- Remaining risks and maintenance triggers are reported.
- The machine-readable QC verdict separates corpus integrity, product architecture, content quality, learning transfer, evidence, and app experience. No narrower pass is described as an overall pass.
