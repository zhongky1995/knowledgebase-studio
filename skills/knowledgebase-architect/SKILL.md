# Knowledgebase Architect

For a course or connected long-form product, use [the structural method](references/structure-method.md). Develop a defensible central judgment, then work out how this reader can arrive at it through evidence, changed understanding and consequential questions. A topic inventory or argument map is useful input; neither determines reading order. Keep the rationale and unresolved choices in the existing design.

Read `references/architecture-modes.md` when selecting between reading, operations, reference or mixed use. Use the content builder's `references/editorial-method.md` to develop the central judgment through actual prose; its continuous examples calibrate explanation level. For evidence synthesis or a system mechanism, use `references/evidence-to-judgment.md` or `references/novice-mental-model.md` as relevant.

A useful structure gives the whole product, its parts and individual chapters distinct responsibilities. Choose the entry and key transitions before treating chapter boundaries as settled. Early explanations can introduce a relationship that later chapters deepen; avoid both premature technical detail and postponing prerequisites the reader already needs. Case-first openings and opportunity-first sequencing are options, not templates. Reference and operating paths follow their own reader use.

Develop structure alongside actual prose and visuals. If an example cannot support a chapter's claim, reconsider the example or chapter responsibility. A directory approved earlier is useful context, not a reason to retain a weak chapter. Consult learning or interface specialists for a current problem, not as an automatic sequence.

Test a consequential structural uncertainty with actual connected prose: the opening and a relevant cross-chapter transition, alongside the full reading route. A successful standalone chapter does not establish its place in the product. Where independent reading is warranted, the controller's [execution route](../structured-knowledgebase-builder/references/independent-execution.md) accepts an ordered `readingPath` and selected full texts, and preserves an overall structure judgment separately from page verdicts. Do not infer unread chapters' quality from headings. An established route needs no ceremonial pilot or repeated user approval.

For an actual migration, inventory the affected old paths and preserve or explicitly relocate their meanings and links before removing them. Ordinary new writing needs no fictional migration map. For a design-only request, unresolved implementation choices can remain labeled; do not demand finished prose, an app or release evidence.

Existing staged projects may own `knowledge-model.json`, `learning-design.json` and `migration-map.yaml`. Reuse their canonical decisions rather than duplicating them. Their legacy validation applies when renewing a whole-stage claim; a scoped editorial change does not silently renew it. See the controller's `references/workflow-protocol.md` for that compatibility route.
